      How to Play

      1. Buy land and construct a building; each costs one action point.
      2. Redevelop only into a higher-cost legal building. The old building contributes a 120% upgrade credit, but never pays cash back.
      3. A brokered sale locks 90% of current property value and settles next turn.
      4. Every securities buy or sell costs one action point and a 1% fee.
      5. Bank borrowing and repayment cost no action points; loans mature in six turns.
      6. Highest net worth after turn 8 wins.
