    Three-Minute Walkthrough

    How to Play

    1. Select a white plot, then buy it for one action point.
    2. Select your plot, choose a building and build for one action point.
    3. Construction becomes active next turn and starts producing income.
    4. Borrowing costs no action point, but every prototype loan is due in six turns.
    5. Watch the city desk: zoning eventually blocks new factories and stores on Residential plots.
    6. Your rival acts after you end the turn. Highest net worth after turn 8 wins.

    Patterns identify ownership: double outline for you, crosshatch for the rival and dotted edges for public land.
